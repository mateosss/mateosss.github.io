Euler angles & gimbal lock

Rotation axes of the gimbals (world frame)

|det [aψ aθ aφ]| = |cos θ|
Why does this matter?

The angular velocity is ω = ψ̇ aψ + θ̇ aθ + φ̇ aφ. When θ = ±90° the yaw and roll axes coincide, so the map from angle rates to ω has rank 2: one direction of rotation (dashed arrow) cannot be produced by any small change of the angles. In optimisation this shows up as a singular Jacobian. Any 3-parameter representation of SO(3) has such singularities somewhere.
